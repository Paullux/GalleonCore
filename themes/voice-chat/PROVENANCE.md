No source image was given. The picture shows generic chat bubbles, a headset, a game controller and a microphone on a purple night sky: no logo, no character, no real person. Released by the author under CC-BY-SA-4.0.

The picture itself carries no logo or character, but the maintainer should either accept this explicitly or replace the wallpaper by one made from a neutral prompt (for example: "purple night sky illustration with chat bubbles, a headset, a microphone and a game controller, no text, 720 x 384 pixels").
